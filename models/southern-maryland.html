SO. MARYLAND CLUBHOUSE

The Southern Maryland Multifamily Clubhouse transforms shared amenity spaces into destinations. From lounge to business center, every room is designed to feel collected and considered, not corporate.

THE BRIEF

Community amenity spaces often default to generic hospitality. The challenge here was to create clubhouse interiors that residents would actually want to spend time in, spaces with the warmth and specificity of a well-designed home rather than a hotel lobby.

DESIGN DECISIONS

The main lounge pairs clean-lined cream seating with a bold black drum coffee table, creating a modern gathering point that feels both relaxed and intentional. Patterned drapery adds texture without overwhelming the neutral palette.

A curated series of black and white photography lines the café seating area, bringing gallery-level sophistication to an everyday amenity. In the business center, sculptural cobalt wall pieces turn a functional workspace into a moment of unexpected art.

THE RESULT

The clubhouse reads as an extension of home rather than an afterthought. Residents use these spaces because they want to, not because they have to. That shift, from obligation to invitation, is what good amenity design achieves.
